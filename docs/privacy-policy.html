MoneyMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

1. Introduction

MoneyMate ("we", "our", or "us") is a couples expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Display name
  • Preferred currency

Expense Data

  • Expense amounts, categories, dates, and notes
  • Budget limits per category
  • Privacy settings per category

Partner Data

  • Partner connection via invite codes
  • Shared expense visibility settings

Technical Data

  • Device push notification tokens (for expense notifications)
  • App usage analytics (anonymized)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the expense tracking service
  • To sync expenses between you and your partner
  • To send push notifications about new expenses
  • To manage your subscription
  • To improve our service

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your expense data is shared only with your connected partner, subject to your privacy settings. We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, cloud functions, push notifications
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management

5. Privacy Controls

MoneyMate gives you granular control over what your partner can see:

  • Share All — Partner sees every transaction in a category
  • Total Only — Partner sees the category total, not individual items
  • Private — Partner cannot see anything in this category

6. Data Security

We use Firebase's built-in security features including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL)
  • Firestore security rules restricting access to couple members only
  • Server-side validation of privacy settings

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Delete Account. Upon deletion:

  • All your personal data is permanently removed
  • Your expenses are deleted from shared records
  • Your partner connection is severed
  • Your Firebase authentication account is deleted

8. Children's Privacy

MoneyMate is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.
