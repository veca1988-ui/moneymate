Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite my partner?

Go to Settings > Invite Partner. Generate a 6-character invite code and share it with your partner. They can enter it in the app under "Join Partner". The code expires in 48 hours.

How do privacy controls work?

Go to Settings > Privacy Controls. For each expense category, you can choose: "Share All" (partner sees everything), "Total Only" (partner sees the total but not individual items), or "Private" (partner cannot see anything).

How do I set a budget?

From the Dashboard, your budget cards are displayed below the spending chart. Tap the + button to add a new budget for any category.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently delete all your data, expenses, and disconnect you from your partner. This action cannot be undone.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Settings > MoneyMate Premium > Restore Purchases. This will restore any active subscription linked to your Apple ID or Google account.

What happens during the free trial?

When you connect with your partner, you get a 14-day free trial of all premium features. After the trial, you can subscribe for $3.99/month or $34.99/year.
